Status: pre-result selection record. No comparative candidate backtest was run or
inspected for this decision. Scores allocate research effort; they do not estimate

## Decision

Prioritise **sector-level cross-sectional momentum** (GICS-sector rotation)
for a preregistered protocol. User-motivated: after CS-01's null (individual-
stock momentum, `not_material_or_not_consistent`), a specific, named
real-world observation ("long chips, short software" over roughly the past
year) that CS-01's flat, ungrouped pooling structurally could not test —
CS-01 never formed a "chips" or "software" group. [`universe_sectors.py`](../../backend/app/universe_sectors.py)
(`0.83.0`) now provides free GICS sector labels, clearing the data blocker
this exact candidate needed.

## Candidate — sector-level cross-sectional momentum

| Field | Pre-result definition |
|---|---|
| Claim | Among 11 GICS sectors, a sector's prior 12-month aggregate return rank predicts its subsequent 1-month aggregate return rank. |
| Scope | S&P 500 members only, `2001-01-01` onward, monthly rebalance. |
| Mechanism | Slow-moving fundamental/thematic cycles (capex, rate, demand cycles) diffuse across many names in a sector simultaneously and persist for months to years — a structurally different, slower mechanism than individual-stock behavioral underreaction. |
| Market-belief proxy | Sector's own trailing 252-session equal-weighted aggregate return. |
| Reality proxy | Same sector's subsequent 21-session equal-weighted aggregate return. |
| Information set | Real point-in-time S&P 500 membership (`members_asof`) intersected with today's GICS classification and real stored price data at each rebalance date. |
| Estimand | Pooled Spearman rank correlation, sector formation rank vs. sector forward rank, via `etf12_rotation_bootstrap` unmodified (11-column synthetic sector-index panel replaces the 12-ETF panel it was built for). |
| Alternatives | Market-beta/regime confound; mega-cap concentration within a sector's aggregate return; today's-GICS-classification bias (a stock reclassified mid-history is attributed to its current sector for its whole price history). |
| Falsifier | Observed correlation at or below the block-permuted null's preregistered one-sided threshold. |
| Data feasibility | `2` (was effectively unscored/blocked before `0.83.0` — no sector labels existed anywhere in this codebase). Point-in-time membership and today's-GICS labels both real and free; the today's-classification (not point-in-time) limitation is disclosed, not a data-readiness blocker (it degrades precision, does not make the estimand unmeasurable). |
| Expressions | Long-short top-minus-bottom-tercile sector rotation overlay (if the correlation clears its floor); `no trade` otherwise — not attempted in this protocol, same claim/trade separation as CS-01. |
| Path and risk | Sector concentration inherently less diversified than an individual-stock book; today's-classification bias contaminates a positive result the same way a look-ahead bias would, via label rather than price. |

## Scorecard

Scale: `0` weak/unavailable, `1` plausible, `2` strong. A zero in data readiness
disqualifies a candidate regardless of total.

| Candidate | Rationale | Product relevance | Distinct information | Data readiness | Implementability | Falsifiability | Research restraint | Diversification | Total | Gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Sector-level cross-sectional momentum | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 2 | 1 | 15 | Prioritise: preregister |

Rationale scores `2`, higher than CS-01's implicit individual-stock rationale:
sector/thematic rotation over multi-month-to-year horizons is a much more
established, mechanistically plausible claim (real capex/rate/demand cycles)
than raw individual-stock price momentum, which CS-01 already showed is
dominated by common-factor noise at this project's chosen design.
Implementability scores `2`, higher than CS-01's cycle-7 implementability
score of `1`: this candidate needs *no new statistical engine* — only a
sector-aggregation step feeding the already-built, already-tested
`etf12_rotation_bootstrap` unmodified. Diversification scores `1`, not `2`:
a sector rotation overlay (if it ever clears its gate) concentrates in
whichever sector currently ranks highest — genuinely less diversified than
CS-01's many-name long-short design, a real tradeoff for the noise
reduction aggregation buys.

## Benchmark suitability

ETF-12 rotation both used — not a funded portfolio benchmark at this stage.

proposed, Passive ETF-12 v1 and SPY remain the relevant funded diagnostics,
per [ADR 0005](../adr/0005-product-objective-and-portfolio-benchmark.md).
