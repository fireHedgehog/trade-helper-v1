# Stage 9A candidate selection — cycle 1

Status: pre-result selection record. No comparative candidate backtest was run or
inspected for this decision. Scores allocate research effort; they do not estimate
alpha or authorise a strategy.

## Decision

Prioritise **Daily Consolidation Zone v1 — support recovery** for a bounded
detector/event-feasibility protocol. Do not implement a trading strategy yet.
Diversified futures trend and cross-sectional equity momentum remain serious but
are parked because the required point-in-time datasets are unavailable. Macro
ideas remain ineligible under ADR 0006. UI rules such as SMA Cross, RSI, Fibonacci,
Wave Pull, and generic breakout are not candidates until independently
operationalised.

The choice is practical, not a claim that support recovery has the strongest
published edge. It is the only serious candidate below with an auditable daily-data
path, a distinct estimand, and a finite falsifiable first experiment.

## Candidate A — consolidation support recovery

| Field | Pre-result definition |
|---|---|
| Claim | After an objectively completed daily consolidation, a later lower-boundary test followed by a same-session close back inside the zone has lower 60-session maximum adverse excursion than matched same-symbol observations. |
| Scope | `SPY, QQQ, IWM, EFA, EEM, TLT, IEF, GLD, DBC, XLK, XLF, XLE`; adjusted daily OHLCV; common history from 2006; no current-constituent equity extension. |
| Mechanism | Repeated trading and attention may anchor participants to a price region; clustered liquidity may create a temporary reaction. This is plausible, not assumed. |
| Market-belief proxy | Completed zone boundaries and the test/recovery event, constructed only from information available by close `t`. |
| Reality proxy | Subsequent 60-session path: maximum adverse excursion, forward return, zone violation, and maximum favourable excursion. |
| Information set | Adjusted OHLCV through completed close `t`; no future-confirmed pivot, revised fundamental, intraday volume profile, or post-event threshold choice. |
| Primary estimand | Median matched-pair difference in 60-session maximum adverse excursion: event minus control. Negative is favourable. |
| Secondary safeguards | Matched-pair forward-return difference and zone-violation probability; lower drawdown cannot be called useful if return is materially sacrificed. |
| Alternatives | Generic trend, recent return, volatility compression, market regime, calendar period, and detector selection can explain the observation. Matching/placebos must address them. |
| Falsifier | The preregistered interval/gate fails to support an economically material reduction in adverse excursion, or feasibility cannot provide adequate independent events/power. |
| Data feasibility | Current ETF daily data is mechanically usable under ADR 0002. Daily volume is not volume-at-price; no HVN claim is permitted. |
| Expressions | First experiment is `no trade`: detector validation and matched event study only. Any later long-only timing overlay requires a separate locked portfolio protocol. |
| Path and risk | Zone gaps, false recovery, clustered overlapping events, concentration by ETF/regime, cash drag, and missed upside. |

Literature supports investigating algorithmic support/resistance but does not
validate this detector or claim: Osler studies published dealer levels in intraday
FX; Chung and Bellotti algorithmically evaluate support/resistance constructions;
Batchelor and Ramyar show that algorithmic Fibonacci zones can separate objective
identification from profitable use. See the source list below.

## Candidate B — diversified futures time-series trend

| Field | Pre-result definition |
|---|---|
| Claim | An instrument's own intermediate-horizon return contains information about its subsequent return across liquid equity-index, bond, currency, and commodity futures; a volatility-scaled diversified overlay may improve portfolio crisis risk. |
| Scope | Point-in-time liquid futures across four asset classes, with executable continuous-contract/roll rules. Exact contracts and dates are unavailable. |
| Mechanism | Gradual information diffusion, behavioural underreaction/herding, or compensation for convex/crash-sensitive positioning. |
| Market-belief proxy | Sign and magnitude of own-contract trailing excess return at decision time. |
| Reality proxy | Subsequent contract excess return and funded overlay return after roll, collateral, leverage, and costs. |
| Information set | Settlement and contract metadata known by close `t`, including contemporaneous expiry/roll eligibility. |
| Estimand | Net active return and crisis drawdown contribution of a diversified overlay versus its preregistered funded benchmark. |
| Alternatives | Volatility scaling, embedded market beta, roll/carry, collateral yield, and long-lived-contract selection. |
| Falsifier | No economically material net active/risk contribution under the locked uncertainty and stability gates. |
| Data feasibility | Unavailable: current data cannot construct point-in-time tradable futures chains, rolls, collateral return, or execution costs. |
| Expressions | Volatility-targeted futures overlay or `no trade`; neither is authorised without the missing data contract. |
| Path and risk | Leverage, gaps, margin, limit moves, roll liquidity, model crowding, reversals, and left-tail losses. |

This is not CTA v1 with tuned parameters. Moskowitz, Ooi, and Pedersen study 58
futures across four asset classes; Hurst, Ooi, and Pedersen extend the historical
evidence. Our current adjusted ETF data and three display-only market-context Yahoo
series cannot reproduce that opportunity set, continuous-contract construction,
roll return, collateral yield, or executable costs. `Data readiness = 0`; park.

## Candidate C — long-only cross-sectional equity momentum

| Field | Pre-result definition |
|---|---|
| Claim | Among a point-in-time investable equity universe, intermediate-horizon past relative-return ranks predict relative subsequent return; a long-only winner sleeve may improve a broad passive alternative after turnover and crash risk. |
| Scope | Point-in-time US equities meeting preregistered price, liquidity, listing, and history rules; delisted and removed securities retained. |
| Mechanism | Gradual information diffusion/underreaction, investor herding, or compensation for crash-sensitive exposure. |
| Market-belief proxy | Cross-sectional rank of trailing intermediate-horizon return, computed from the eligible universe at `t`. |
| Reality proxy | Subsequent relative and funded portfolio return after turnover, delistings, and costs. |
| Information set | Prices, corporate actions, membership, listings, and liquidity known at formation time; no current-constituent reconstruction. |
| Estimand | Net active return and risk of a long-only winner sleeve versus a matched broad passive benchmark. |
| Alternatives | Size/industry/market exposure, stale prices, liquidity, survivorship, and short-term reversal. |
| Falsifier | No economically material net active return/risk improvement under the locked uncertainty, crash, and stability gates. |
| Data feasibility | Unavailable: the current survivor list has no historical membership, delistings, or point-in-time eligibility. |
| Expressions | Long-only top-rank sleeve, tilted benchmark, or `no trade`; the foundational long-short result does not validate these expressions. |
| Path and risk | Momentum crashes, crowding, turnover, concentration, sector drift, gaps, and benchmark mismatch. |

Jegadeesh and Titman provide the foundational long-short evidence, but a
long-only expression is a different portfolio claim and must be benchmarked
accordingly.

The current roughly 500-symbol survivor list lacks historical membership,
delistings, and point-in-time eligibility. The 12-ETF set is too small and
structurally heterogeneous for the intended equity cross-section. `Data readiness =
0`; park rather than substitute the current list.

## Benchmark suitability

The feasibility/event study does not compare funded portfolios, so Passive ETF-12
v1 is not its primary benchmark. Its primary comparator is a preregistered matched
same-symbol non-event observation. Matching must use only pre-event market trend,
recent return, volatility, and calendar information.

If the event survives and an executable overlay is proposed, Passive ETF-12 v1 is
a relevant primary funded comparator because the candidate uses the same 12 ETFs.
Constant exposure per symbol, SPY, and cash remain diagnostics. Any different
universe or weighting requires a new benchmark suitability decision before results.

## Next locked deliverable

Write one detector/event-feasibility protocol before computing zone counts or
outcomes. It must freeze:

1. one zone-boundary algorithm and a small declared detector grid;
2. event de-duplication, matching, purge/embargo, and placebo construction;
3. the economically relevant adverse-excursion effect and return-sacrifice limit;
4. prospective event-count/precision or power assessment and `not evaluable` rule;
5. the family/trial inventory and dependence-aware correction;
6. development/confirmation contamination boundaries;
7. deterministic specification/data fingerprints and artifact schema.

No chart marker, rank, entry rule, stop, portfolio backtest, or parameter tuning is
authorised by this selection record.
